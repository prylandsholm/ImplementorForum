# About Semantic4iop.org

## Mission
The Semantic4iop.org Implementor Forum is dedicated to advancing industrial semantic interoperability through collaborative development of standards, best practices, and implementation guidance.

## Purpose
Our community serves to:
- Share knowledge and experiences in implementing semantic interoperability
- Develop and test semantic standards for industrial applications
- Provide a platform for industry, academia, and standards bodies to collaborate
- Accelerate the adoption of semantic interoperability across sectors

## 2026 Summit
The Industrial Semantic Interoperability Summit 2026 brought together leading experts and practitioners to:
- Present current implementations and use cases
- Discuss technical challenges and solutions
- Plan the future direction of semantic interoperability standards
- Build a collaborative community
